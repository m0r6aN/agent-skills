## New cross-parcel ruling

**T-35 (amends T-1; applies to P1a and P1b and every later parcel):** identifier alphabet for `role_id`, `role_generation`, `profile_id`, `session_id`, `task_id` is `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,128}$` (no `:`, `,`, whitespace or control characters). The grant-scope grammar is P1b's: `role:{profile_id}:{role_id}`, `profile:{profile_id}:shared`, `op:legacy`, with each id component in the alphabet above. **The Gateway rejects (fails closed) any binding whose ids or grant scopes violate this** at binding resolution (JWT claims and enrollment records); **Cortex rejects (403) any carriage header that violates it**. Neither side may silently drop or rewrite a malformed value into a valid one. Test fixtures on both sides use grammar-conformant values.
