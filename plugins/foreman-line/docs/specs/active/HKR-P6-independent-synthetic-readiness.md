# HKR-P6 — Independent Synthetic Readiness

## Intent

Independently assess the exact integrated synthetic pipeline and produce a bounded readiness decision for the owner. Stop at an evidence-backed recommendation; neither a green assessment nor this draft authorizes real-history processing.

## Constraints

- P0–P5 must have exact accepted revisions, independently verified results and explicit unresolved findings. A coordinator consumes verification, not self-produced green evidence; a builder cannot verify its own completion claim.
- Exercise the integrated selected local path, not only isolated unit tests. Mocks/fakes are labeled and cannot stand in for physical isolation, durable restart behavior, real adapter accounting or authentic Runtime/epoch proof.
- All mandatory synthetic cases and hard stop/control conditions must pass without unauthorized disclosure, hidden skips or unsupported success. A blocked/failed assessment is valid reporting but not successful readiness or a waiver.
- S1 source eligibility/rights, actual project/profile grants and four-real-conversation evaluation remain unproven. NP-13–NP-17, G6/G7 and production/canonical admission are excluded, not silently satisfied or made prerequisites to this candidate-only synthetic goal.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Independent verification reproduces the approved integrated synthetic NP-01–NP-11 case matrix and NP-12 evaluation-structure checks on pinned allowed artifacts. Report each pass/fail/blocked result with actual evidence, tested identity/store/route/budget and any simulated boundary. No required failure/unknown/skip becomes green.
2. Independent adversarial review addresses source instructions, cross-domain/assignment read paths, metadata/cache leakage, stale derivatives/reassignment, lost acknowledgements/restart, cumulative budget exhaustion and reference contamination. Findings are triaged by the accountable coordinator; changed locked decisions require owner ratification, not in-review fixes.
3. A final evidence index, actual copy/retention/cleanup inventory and limitation/gate report recommends ready, blocked or failed within synthetic scope. It names the additional approvals and proofs needed for any S1 step, records no ingestion/publication permission and ends with no scheduled continuation or new source access. Successful synthetic readiness requires all applicable controls actually demonstrated, not just a completed report.

## Out of Scope

- Fixing implementation during verification, merging code or changing another goal's scope/ownership.
- Acquiring/processing real histories, actual tenant/profile enrollment, shared recall or Cortex publication.
- Authentic service/receipt/epoch certification, real-source quality metrics, expanding platforms or enabling personal memory/dreams.

## Dependencies And Promotion Blockers

Accepted P0–P5 and permitted integrated-test scope are required. Actual independent verifiers/reviewers, exact evidence paths, eligible approved review routes and controlled rerun budgets remain UNBOUND. No council/review session is launched here. Review policy and authorization must be supplied by the separately ratified future goal. Shaping surfaces require amendment if future non-doc verification tooling is needed.

## Allowed Files

No test harness, report, evidence index or custody/cleanup output path is authorized by this draft. P0 must propose exact read-only targets and permitted report mutations, with independent-verifier ownership, for ratification before activation. Verification may not repair source files or generate evidence in unapproved paths.

## Verification Plan

Use independent deterministic execution evidence and the future goal's required fresh adversarial review chain. Architecture/risk parcels require the applicable independently authorized review depth. The owner decides whether to accept the resulting readiness recommendation and separately authorize any later phase.

Mandated reviewer questions: Is the integrated path really the one tested? Are mock/authentic and synthetic/real claims unmistakable? Is any outstanding failure hidden by a stage label? Does the final report stop before acquiring history? Can the retained artifacts or cleanup process leak held content or reset control state?
