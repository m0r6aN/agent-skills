# EXA-P6 - Prove and activate the scoped execution path for the blocked sessions

## Intent

Close the original blocker with independently verified, real bounded sessions through the installed CLI. Distinguish static conformance, public synthetic execution, purpose-specific internal admission and operational usefulness.

## Constraints

These are proposed implementation boundaries in a Stage A draft. Human choices confirmed in this chat: local CLI first; only DocSpine/Kompress shaping and AI Council critique sessions; select concrete routes during admission from approved profiles, with no fixed roster in these specs. This draft grants no implementation, provider spend, dispatch, merge, or activation authority. Parcel identifiers are local planning keys, not claims of registered Jira issues. Risk classifications are recommendations for coordinator/human disposition.

Only this parcel's separately approved live proof may invoke providers. First run default deterministic conformance with network-incapable providers and actual stores/runtime assembly. Pin tested source/runtime/installations and reconcile required CI. Refresh current provider capability/data/auth/billing evidence through its owning authority; do not infer it from old smoke tests.

Require an explicit owner-issued live grant before any probe, including actual packet/source identity, provider/data/auth eligibility, maximum attempts/tokens/output/reasoning/requests/time, money/quota limits and stop/cleanup conditions. Model identities are chosen during admission from approved profiles. A public synthetic probe does not approve internal packets or a different provider/auth mode.

Independent architecture/security/process reviewers must be admitted through the P0 bootstrap disposition; the new launcher and its author cannot authorize or accept themselves. If required reviewers/control evidence are unavailable, report that precise gate. Do not label this a passing review on the basis of local tests.

After independent acceptance and purpose-specific internal admission, run the two actual shaping task packets and required Council critique packets. Require at least two family-independent critique successes plus orchestrator, and preserve any stricter existing architecture/risk review requirement. Every seat has a distinct authenticated route/permit and actual runtime observation.

The evidence bundle includes baseline/installation, approved packet/profile/route/permit refs, request and attempt state, observed identity/thinking, findings/result refs, usage/billing/quota observations and uncertainties, independent review dispositions and rollback instructions. It contains no credentials or unrelated history. A named legacy approved route receipt and an EXA companion authorization remain explicitly different formats.

Do not convert actual result records into Stage A genesis/approval/closure receipts. Coordinator handles acceptance and downstream stage artifacts under their own contracts.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Deterministic conformance covers malformed/forged/stale/cross-task approval, packet changes, identity/auth/thinking mismatch, evidence escapes, concurrency/replay, allowance exhaustion, crash uncertainty, cancellation and legacy refusal preservation.

2. Black-box installed CLI tests run from the actual calling environment with matched dependencies and supported Node; tested package/runtime pins are recorded.

3. An owner-approved public synthetic live probe verifies a real provider request, actual executed identity, bounded tools/resources/usage, result custody and cleanup. It is labelled public-only evidence.

4. Required independent architecture/security/process review evaluates the exact implementation and platform evidence; findings are dispositioned, and material changes repeat relevant validation.

5. Authentic current admission covers the actual data and purpose of DocSpine, Kompress and each Council seat; no inherited OPMEM exception or model roster shortcut.

6. The CLI completes separate docspine-shaping and kompress-shaping sessions with usable task-specific findings/draft artifacts and distinct route/attempt evidence.

7. The required independent Council critique seats complete under their own approvals and aggregate limits; coordinator produces a synthesis grounded in actual seat outputs and reports dissent/missing coverage.

8. A final blocker-to-evidence table marks each original blocker resolved or held. Only complete useful sessions plus accepted evidence justify UNBLOCKED; refusal-only commands, fixture receipts or smoke OK cannot.

9. Rollback disables new admissions and revokes outstanding permits, preserves uncertain liabilities/evidence and leaves existing policies/configurations unchanged. A reversible rollout drill is evidenced within approved scope.

## Out of Scope

Production deployment, go-to-market publication, full-pantheon readiness certification, fixes to DocSpine/Kompress services, new provider accounts/keys, billing changes, additional model surfaces and waiver of independent review.
