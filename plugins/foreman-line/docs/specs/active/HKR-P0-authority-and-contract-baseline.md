# HKR-P0 — Authority and Contract Baseline

## Intent

Turn the reconciled narrow pilot into a reviewable synthetic-only implementation contract without changing its architecture or enrolling real sources. Identify the precise ownership, compatibility and control seams a future coordinator must ratify before any builder can run. This is a documents-only future parcel; this file itself is only a draft.

## Constraints

- Follow the approved shaping record. Scope ends at independently verified synthetic readiness; no real-history processing or canonical publication.
- Read only explicitly authorized canon/code during a future P0; preserve existing dirty changes, goal owners and frozen council artifacts. Current applicable contracts outrank the bounded historical discovery snapshot.
- Do not invent missing APIs, choose a second accepted-memory store, silently adopt another goal's grants or amend frozen Foreman/service contracts. Unsupported required seams cause a hold and owner disposition.
- Exact production identities, endpoints, credentials, source locators and real payloads stay out of repo specs. Permission-profile omission in this draft is deliberate; dispatch requires an independently approved actual envelope.
- Risk/routing classifications are owner-approved for drafting, not an approved provider/model route. Planning aliases are not registered issues.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The baseline identifies approved authoritative design revisions and current code/canon references; maps requirement ownership, dependencies on existing goals and explicit conflicts/unsupported seams; and records source restrictions without inspecting real histories. The owner disposes load-bearing open choices before freeze.
2. Versioned synthetic contracts define exact-span evidence/source identity, clocks/origin grouping, candidate states/dependencies, grants/release checks, durable compare-and-commit/recovery semantics and bounded extraction/review interfaces. Each field has a trusted producer, consumer and failure behavior. No synthetic issuer is represented as authentic Runtime/epoch proof.
3. A case/coverage matrix assigns NP-01–NP-11 and synthetic NP-12 structure to parcels with observable expected results and independent verification, while recording NP-12 real-source and NP-13–NP-17 exclusions. Concrete implementation/toolchain/isolation/store/route choices, exact downstream Allowed Files/evidence paths and a separately proposed S0 budget/retention envelope are ready for owner ratification. P0 cannot approve its own proposals or promote downstream drafts.

## Out of Scope

- Implementing parsers, databases, enforcement, inference, fixtures or services; executing even synthetic cases.
- Real conversation selection validation, source copying, transcript hashes, hidden reasoning capture or content inspection.
- Resuming, changing or taking ownership of existing memory/Collective goals; Gateway/Cortex/Runtime/AgentTask production changes.
- New numerical gravity, empirical quality claims, canonical promotion or broad platform expansion.
