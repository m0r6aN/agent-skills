TOP 3 BRUTAL FINDINGS
1. 3 of the 13 hash pins are wrong, and the linter still passed. loop-directive has 63 hex chars (an "a" is missing from "db2fea36"), terminal-receipt-settlement has 65 (an extra "f" in "cbffb797"), and canonicalization has uppercase "DECC22BDD" where the real hash is lowercase. Builder Step 0 would stop every time, and the "drift accepted, pin re-based" ruling points at a hash that does not exist.
2. The contract leaves the real design to the builder. It never gives a table, column, key or unique-constraint for reservations, pending-effect state, pre-effect receipts, terminals or settlements. Only "sufficient for P2B" is required, so reviewers cannot test for sufficiency, and P2B inherits a schema no one approved. Row 268 also says "P2A/P2B transaction", but P2A has no transaction.
3. Governance and handoff wording can be misread. "Gate 2 (beyond this parcel's own dispatch)" reads as if this parcel's own Gate 2 is already granted. The carried-items list names P2A as an owner of the key provider, yet P2A must not choose one. The handoff relies on a "keon-systems commit message", but no line grants commit authority. The rollback step "git checkout --" writes to a shared index. The multi-process and failure-atomic migration proofs have no fault-injection seam inside the two Allowed Files.
