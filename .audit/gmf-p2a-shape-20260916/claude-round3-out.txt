1. 3. Scope/authority leak: PendingEffectResolutions(ResolutionKind) freezes P2C quarantine/release shape (P1-C113, explicitly Out of Scope), UNIQUE(PermissionId,SpendIndex) silently decides retry-per-spend without P1-C106 replay/retry linkage, and frontmatter surfaces list only agent-skills shaping files while prose makes keon-systems the sole builder authority.
